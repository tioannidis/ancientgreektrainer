Ancient Greek Trainer helps you learn the declension of Ancient Greek nouns.

Type the requested case and number of a noun, for example the genitive plural of a word. The app checks your answer right away and tells you whether the form, the accent and the article are correct, and shows the full paradigm when you need it.

Features:
- 122 nouns covering all 14 noun types of the first, second and third declension
- Filter by declension and by case
- Optional question for the article
- Full paradigm table with singular and plural
- Greek keyboard for diacritics, or type with your own keyboard
- Streak, accuracy and statistics; words you get wrong come up more often
- Dark, light or system theme
- German and English interface, following the system language by default

The app contains no ads, performs no tracking, requires no network access and asks for no permissions. It is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.
